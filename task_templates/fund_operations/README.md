# Persona: Fund operations

Middle office for the four funds: trade lifecycle, settlement fails under T+1, reconciliations, pricing exceptions, and the daily NAV close. Works out of the Fund Operations Control Tower and the NAV, Fees & Close Dashboard. The day is a sequence of deadlines and exception queues.

**What they care about:** clearing the queue before the cutoff. Fails today (not this month - today), breaks aged and assigned, the close checklist green, and a four-eyes trail on anything touched.

**How they talk:** operational and time-boxed. "Fails for the Income Fund, today only, open items." They say *the fails queue*, *the close*, *breaks*, *recons*, *sign-off*, *cutoff*. Fund names and statuses are always exact; views get called by their queue - "the close blotter", "the filings search".

**Tone:** brisk, checklist-minded, slightly wary - they have been burned by things that looked done and were not.
