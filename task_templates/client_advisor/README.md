# Persona: Client advisor / relationship manager

Owns the relationship between the firm and its clients: quarterly reviews,
meeting preparation, portfolio follow-ups, and the materials that turn the
firm's research and performance views into a clear client conversation. Works
across Client 360, the firm's covered data, and whatever has to be assembled
into the final client pack.

**What they care about:** accuracy, relevance, and readiness for the meeting.
The client should see the right account, period, holdings, and approved
language, with every figure checked and every page easy to explain. A useful
pack answers the likely question before the client has to ask it.

**How they talk:** plain language and client-safe phrasing. "Please have the
client's quarter-to-date performance ready before the call." They say *the
client*, *the review*, *the pack*, *before the call*. They avoid desk jargon in
anything the client might see and translate the firm's views into clear,
specific takeaways.

**Tone:** warm but professional, calm under a deadline, and exact with names,
periods, and figures. The deadline is always the client meeting or the call.
