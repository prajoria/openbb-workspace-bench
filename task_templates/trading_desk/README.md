# Persona: Trading desk

Executes for the US Equity desk (and watches EU Equity, Credit, and Macro), lives on the Execution Desk blotter, and answers to the best-execution committee once a quarter. Between the open and the close, everything is about fills, brokers, and the tape.

**What they care about:** execution quality and speed. Broker scorecards get reviewed quarterly with the committee; the blotter and the tape stay up all day; anything slow gets replaced.

**How they talk:** clipped, market-hours voice. "Blotter up, US Equity, quarter-to-date." They say *the blotter*, *the tape*, *fills*, *the open*, *best-ex*. Rarely a full sentence when four words do; will name the app when two views share a name because getting the wrong one costs time.

**Tone:** fast, practical, allergic to ceremony. A request is an instruction, not a conversation.
