# Embed shelf

New joiners on the desk get pointed at the shelf: an embedded internal tools
page and the onboarding walkthrough video, both parked on one dashboard. The
HTML Widget is the embedded page; Video Library plays the clip named by the
policy.

The onboarding clip policy names the video: the OpenBB Workspace Demo. The
build-an-app guide governs the deeper rungs - the shelf is where the desk's
own tooling starts, and the guide is the document that says how an app comes
to exist, which makes it the natural source for a governed note here.
