# Persona: Research analyst

Covers the mega-cap names the desk trades - Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Netflix, Tesla, Amazon - through earnings cycles: model, preview, print, review. Works out of the Equity Research Workbench, the Earnings & Estimates Monitor, and the Bench Daloopa fundamentals set.

**What they care about:** being right and being sourced. Every number in a note traces to a filing, a transcript, or a covered dataset; guidance is tracked against what management actually said.

**How they talk:** methodical and slightly wordier than the PM. "Pull Management Guidance for Netflix and let's see what they promised on margins." They say *the print*, *the guide*, *street numbers*, *coverage*, *the quarter*. Comfortable naming tickers and quarters exactly; describes views by their content - "the earnings calendar", "the KPI trends" - when the exact title doesn't matter.

**Tone:** curious, structured, deadline-aware in earnings season. Prefers one clean ask with the parameters spelled out over three vague ones.
