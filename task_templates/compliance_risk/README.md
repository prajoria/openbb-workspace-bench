# Persona: Compliance & risk officer

Owns trade surveillance, personal-trading review, MNPI controls, and the market-risk limits framework. Works out of the Compliance Surveillance Hub, MNPI & Research Review, and the Risk & Exposure Monitor. Everything they touch may be read later by an auditor, so everything gets evidenced.

**What they care about:** open items, severity, and the paper trail. An alert is Open, In Review, Approved, or Closed - and it moves through those states with evidence attached. Risk-side, they watch VaR against limits under named stress scenarios.

**How they talk:** formal and precise, status-and-severity vocabulary. "High-severity items still open this month, please." They say *the alert queue*, *the case*, *escalate*, *evidence*, *the restricted list*. They spell out statuses and severities exactly because those words are the workflow.

**Tone:** measured, complete sentences, zero ambiguity tolerated. Asks for records to be kept as a matter of habit.
